## AI Automation System Requirement

The website should be extended in a secure, structured way without rebuilding the storefront or replacing the existing data model. The existing customer-facing site and its inventory, product records, customer flows, and policies should remain the system of record.

### Goal

Build an AI-powered internal administration and automation layer that supports store operations with minimal manual effort while preserving the current storefront, URLs, SEO, customer experience, order workflow, and deployment setup.

### Mandatory Principles

- Extend the current website rather than replacing it.
- Keep one source of truth for products, inventory, orders, and customers.
- Do not invent product facts, stock, authenticity claims, or delivery guarantees.
- Require explicit authorization for sensitive operations such as cancellations, refunds, payment-status updates, and major pricing changes.
- Keep AI recommendations and automation in draft or review mode whenever content or business actions are not yet approved.
- Never claim a task or action succeeded unless the backend confirms the result.

## Functional Areas for the AI Admin Layer

### 1. AI Command Center

Create an admin-side assistant with:

- natural language chat for store instructions
- product and order context
- action previews before execution
- approval, rejection, editing, and retry flows
- run logs and task status tracking
- scheduled automation management
- usage and cost monitoring
- searchable action history

Example administrator commands:

- Summarize today's orders and revenue.
- Find products with missing descriptions, tags, or SEO metadata.
- Prepare product listings using uploaded product images.
- Identify low-stock products.
- Draft a festival promotion using in-stock items.
- Highlight products that have not sold recently.
- Generate a weekly business report.
- Show pending tasks that require approval.

### 2. Product Management AI

The AI should be allowed to:

- read product data and product metadata
- draft names, descriptions, features, and tags
- suggest categories, themes, materials, and care instructions
- propose SEO titles and meta descriptions
- flag missing values, duplicates, inconsistent pricing, and weak product content
- check inventory before campaign inclusion
- prepare draft product updates
- update only validated and authorized fields

It must not invent details that are not backed by the database or the product record itself.

### 3. Inventory and Order Automation

The AI system should help with:

- available stock monitoring
- low-stock alerting
- restocking suggestions based on actual sales and inventory history
- stock adjustment logs
- negative-stock prevention
- order summarization by status
- fulfillment problem detection
- customer notification drafts
- payment verification checks

Financial operations such as refunds, cancellations, and manual payment changes must require explicit approval.

### 4. Customer Support Assistant

Build a support assistant that can:

- answer product questions
- explain care, shipping, return, and customization policy
- look up order data only after appropriate authentication
- draft replies in English and Nepali, with Maithili support where practical
- identify urgent or disputed customer issues
- escalate uncertain cases to an admin
- maintain support interaction records

This assistant should never expose private customer information, credentials, or internal business records to unauthorized users.

### 5. Marketing and SEO Automation

Provide campaign and optimization tools for:

- product SEO audits
- missing metadata detection
- internal link suggestions
- social caption drafting
- festival and campaign planning
- promotional calendar generation
- product recommendation logic using real stock and sales data
- analytics and performance reporting

Campaign drafts should remain unapproved by default. The AI must not trigger ad changes, content publishing, pricing changes, or discount publishing without proper admin approval and verified integrations.

### 6. Scheduled Background Operations

The system should support background jobs like:

- daily sales summaries
- low-stock monitoring
- listing quality checks
- weekly SEO reports
- incomplete order alerts
- failed integration checks
- weekly business summaries

All scheduled tasks should log results, support retries, and maintain clear action history.

## Data and Security Expectations

- Use the existing backend and database through authenticated, explicitly authorized functions.
- Keep admin-only features protected behind permission checks.
- Do not expose internal secrets, API keys, admin routes, or raw database access to unauthenticated users.
- Validate all generated content against the real product schema and database constraints.
- Preserve the existing storefront, URL structure, SEO setup, and user flows.
